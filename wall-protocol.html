THE WALL PROTOCOL

You hit a wall.
Here's the process.

Don't stop. Don't give up. Don't do half the work. Follow this checklist every time — extract the task, check it off, get AI to finish it.

↓ scroll to begin

Step 0 — Launch AI Immediately

🔍
Mac: ⌘ Space → type "claude" → Enter  |  iOS: Swipe down from home → type "claude" → tap

Or open Terminal and type: claude

The 6-Step Wall Protocol

Click each step when complete. Don't skip. Order matters.

✓
STEP 01
Name the wall
Write one sentence: "I got stuck because ___." Don't skip this. Naming it unlocks the fix.
✓
STEP 02
Extract the checklist
Copy all tasks, notes, half-done steps into the checklist below. Get everything out of your head and into the list.
✓
STEP 03
Identify the blocker
Which single item is blocking everything else? Mark it. That's the only task that matters right now.
✓
STEP 04
Hand it to AI
⌘Space → Claude → paste the blocker task. Don't explain. Just paste the task. Let AI unstick you.
✓
STEP 05
Check off + keep moving
Once unstuck: check it off. Move to next item. Don't revisit finished tasks. Forward only.
✓
STEP 06
Log what broke + why
One line in ~/AI-OS/logs/claude-thinking-log.md: what the wall was + what broke it. Future-you will thank you.

Claude Prompts — Use When Stuck

stuck on task
I'm stuck on: [paste task]. Give me option 1 or option 2 to unblock. I will pick one and execute immediately.
half done
I started this and hit a wall: [describe]. Here's what's done: [list]. Finish it for me — complete every step, don't stop halfway.
error
I got this error: [paste error]. File: [path]. Fix it, test it, and tell me what caused it so I don't hit this again.
lost context
Read ~/AI-OS/logs/claude-thinking-log.md and ~/.claude/LAST_SESSION_SUMMARY.md. Tell me where I left off and what the next step is.
no internet
Run: echo "I'm offline" | ollama run llama3.2 — then route all tasks through Ollama until reconnected.
