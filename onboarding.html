Share this page with anyone who needs to use Claude. It covers setup, first prompts, and what to do when you hit a wall. No experience needed.

🔍 Step 1 — Launch Claude (Mac)

1

Press ⌘ Space to open Spotlight

2

Type claude and press Enter

3

Or open Terminal and type claude — Claude Code CLI starts

📱 Step 1 — Launch Claude (iPhone/iPad)

1

Swipe down from home screen to open Search

2

Type Claude → tap the app

3

Or go to claude.ai in Safari → Add to Home Screen for one-tap access

📋 Step 2 — Create Your Log File

1

In Claude, type this exact prompt:

Create a file at ~/my-log.md and write today's date as the header. I will use this to log what I'm working on and what AI helped me do.
2

After each session, ask Claude: "Add a one-line summary of what we just did to ~/my-log.md"

⚡ Step 3 — First Prompts to Try

Show me what files are on my Desktop and tell me what each one is for
Read ~/my-log.md and tell me what I was working on last time
I need to [describe task]. Break it into steps, then do each one. Don't stop halfway.

🧱 Step 4 — When You Hit A Wall

1

Don't give up. Don't close the window. Don't start over.

2

Copy this prompt and paste it into Claude:

I hit a wall. Here's what I was trying to do: [describe]. Here's where I got stuck: [describe]. Give me option 1 or option 2 to fix it. I'll pick one and you'll execute it immediately.
3

Claude will give you 2 options. Pick one. Don't overthink. Execute.

4

If Claude says it can't do something — say "Find another way" and it will.

✅ Rules

→

Never do half a task — if Claude stops early, say "Keep going, finish it"

→

If you get no response for 10 min — Claude will auto-proceed. Don't wait on it.

→

Log every session — future you depends on it

→

When stuck: checklist → blocker → AI → check off → move on
