Lead Capture
Facebook Lead Form — 7 Questions

Leads are collected inside Facebook — no external landing page needed. Every submission syncs to GHL as a new opportunity.

1
What is your name? (First / Last)
2
What is your phone number?
3
What city are you located in?
4
Who needs care? (Myself / Parent / Spouse / Other family member)
5
How old is the person who needs care?
6
What type of support do they need? (Personal care / Companionship / Housekeeping / Not sure)
7
How soon do you need help? (ASAP / Within 1 month / Just researching)
