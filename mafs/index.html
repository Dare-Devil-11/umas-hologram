How It Works

Getting Help Is Simple

Three easy steps to connect with the support your family deserves — no cost, no judgment, no barriers.

1

Tell Us Your Need

Complete our short intake form in under 2 minutes. Share what your family is facing so we can match you with the right program.

2

We Reach Out

A MAFS care coordinator will contact you within 24 hours to confirm your needs and schedule your first appointment.

3

Get the Support

Begin services right away — in person or via telehealth. No waitlists for crisis services. Bilingual staff available.
